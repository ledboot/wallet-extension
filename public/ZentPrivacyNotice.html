Zent Wallet Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Zent Wallet ("we", "our", or "us") is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet that puts you in complete control of your digital assets. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our wallet services, applications, and websites.

By using Zent Wallet, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, screen resolution, and other technical information
  • Usage Data: Interaction logs, feature usage patterns, session duration, pages viewed, and error reports
  • Performance Data: Application performance metrics, crash reports, and diagnostic information
  • Network Information: Blockchain node connections, transaction submission data, and sync status

2.2 Wallet Data

The following wallet-related data may be processed:

  • Public Addresses: Your wallet addresses and transaction history (publicly available on blockchain)
  • Transaction Metadata: Transaction timestamps, amounts, and counterparties
  • Token Balances: Current balances of your digital assets
  • Network Preferences: Your preferred blockchain networks and gas settings

2.3 Optional Information

We may collect information you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact Information: Email address or other contact details for support
  • Feedback: User feedback, bug reports, and feature requests
  • Preferences: Custom settings and personalization choices

3. What We DO NOT Collect

🔒 Critical Security Information

Zent Wallet is designed with privacy-first principles. We NEVER collect or have access to:

  • Private Keys: Your private keys never leave your device
  • Seed Phrases: Mnemonic phrases are never transmitted or stored on our servers
  • Passwords: Your wallet passwords are never collected or stored
  • Asset Control: You have full control over your digital assets at all times

Zent Wallet is a non-custodial wallet. This means we cannot access, freeze, or move your funds without your explicit permission and private keys.

5. Blockchain Transparency

⛓️ Public Nature of Blockchain

Important: Transactions on public blockchains are inherently transparent and cannot be deleted or altered. Your wallet addresses and transaction history are publicly visible to anyone on the blockchain.

This means:

  • Your wallet addresses and balances may be visible to blockchain explorers
  • Transaction history is permanently recorded on the blockchain
  • Third parties may analyze blockchain data for various purposes
  • We cannot hide or delete your transaction history from the blockchain
